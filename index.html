The Mission — Bolti Duniya
“Every book should speak — in every language — for everyone who cannot read.”

Around 750 million adults worldwide cannot read. Bolti Duniya (“The Speaking World”) is Loona Automation's 10-year mission to change that with AI: photograph a page, and the book explains itself aloud, in the listener's own language. The mission starts where the need is closest to home — Pakistan's school textbooks.

Two live products already carry the mission: Loona AI Notes is the brain — structured knowledge from real official textbooks — and Loona Bolti Kitab is the voice — turning any photographed page into a simple spoken explanation.
